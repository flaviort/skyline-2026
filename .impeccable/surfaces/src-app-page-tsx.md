# Homepage surface brief

Scope: `/` (homepage). Visitor mode: Persuade. Built one part at a time with user approval per part (docs/PLAN.md).

Audience and job: marketing leads, founders and owners deciding in under a minute whether Skyline is worth a conversation. Action: contact (menu button, contact CTA). Proof: 6 real case studies exported from the old WordPress, client logos from the user, office photos. Constraint: layout, spacing, type rhythm and motion follow extrafazant.nl by user request; no reuse of its copy, logo, fonts or code; reference media only as gitignored dev placeholders. English only, fully static.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A Dallas agency that grows brands like a space mission, told in the reference's grammar: centered stacks of heavy grotesk caps where one line drops to a hairline weight, on a black ground (changed from white after the first review). Refuses the old dark-starfield 3D look and the agency default of hero plus stats plus icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Ink #101010 ground with off-white #f4f4f4 text, international orange #ff4f00 for logo, arrow chips and primary buttons. One typeface: Inter Tight bold caps at 0.8 leading against a hairline accent line of the same family. Media in thick solid color frames, tilted a few degrees. Sticker-style mission patches. Nova, the white-suited astronaut, floats through the banner as a live 3D character and appears in stickers.

STORY: The visitor learns Skyline is a full-service growth agency in Dallas, sees brands it has worked with, sees real projects stacked in front of them, grasps three service pillars, and starts a conversation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Logo mark top-left, centered white pill nav, Contact button top-right. Centered serif lead, two lines, about 36px at 1440. Headline WE GROW / BRANDS / ASTRONOMICALLY at 144px, last line serif. Discover more squiggle link with orange arrow chip. Client logo marquee on the bottom edge. A rigged 3D Nova floats in from the top or a side and keeps floating around the screen, wandering when idle, gently following the mouse with his whole body, doing idle tricks, moving aside over links; he floats out on scroll and returns at the bottom of the page, upper half peeking from the bottom middle, waving goodbye. Primary action: Contact in the nav.

FORM: brief-pinned (extrafazant.nl homepage grammar, requested by the user). No concept-seed roll; seed key: none.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Memorable moment: Nova following the mouse in 3D in the first viewport; then the pinned stack of framed project cards on the dark band, with the rotating THIS IS HOW WE ORBIT badge; second, the stickers trailing the mouse in the contact CTA.

Part 6: Brands we have launched (project images with hand-lettered client names), turning into Nova was here as Nova art arrives.

Unresolved: custom cursors, Nova rig fixes and optional clips from the designer, goodbye on other pages.
